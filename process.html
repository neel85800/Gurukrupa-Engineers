How it works

From enquiry to dispatch

  1. Enquiry

    Send a drawing, a photo of the worn part, or simply the chain number, tooth count and shaft size.

  2. Material

    Grade chosen for the duty — mild steel for light work, EN8 or EN9 where the drive is loaded or abrasive.

  3. Tooth cutting

    Teeth cut to the chain standard, with rows kept in register on duplex and triplex wheels.

  4. Machining

    Bore, keyway, hub and grub screws finished to your fitting, or left pilot bored on request.

  5. Hardening

    Tooth flanks induction hardened where specified, then checked for hardness and distortion.

  6. Inspection

    Runout, pitch and bore verified, part cleaned and oiled, then packed and dispatched.
