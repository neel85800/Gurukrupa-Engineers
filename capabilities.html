Capabilities

Cut, machined, hardened, checked

Everything a sprocket needs between raw stock and a shaft, under one roof.

Tooth cutting

Teeth cut to the chain series you specify, with the pitch circle and seating radius held to the standard so the chain seats properly and wears evenly.

Turning & boring

Bores finished to size, keyways cut, hubs faced and grub screws tapped. Pilot bored for your own finishing where you prefer to do it yourself.

Heat treatment

Induction hardened teeth where the drive runs fast or dirty, so the tooth flanks last without making the whole wheel brittle.

Fabricated wheels

Large diameter chain wheels built up from plate and boss, welded and stress relieved, for sizes where cutting from solid makes no sense.

Inspection

Runout, pitch and bore checked before anything leaves. Hardness verified on heat treated parts.

Repair & re-toothing

Worn wheels re-toothed or re-bushed where the body is still sound — often faster and cheaper than a new part.
